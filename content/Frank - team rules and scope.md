# Frank - team rules and scope

> [!info] Context
> I asked Frank to lead the discussion in an effort to find a solution to the problem of a lack of commitment to the work on the part of several group members, and to determine how we should proceed if they decided not to cooperate.
>
> The professor assigned us a group Feedpulse assignment that received a very high grade because he felt the topics covered were well-focused, presented with complete clarity, and were important. He told us that it would be best if only one person from the group posted a comment so we wouldn't end up with a jumble of messages.

> [!quote] What they said
> Frank guided us through several very important points that we hadn't paid much attention to at work. For example:
>
> - He mentioned the need to establish group rules regarding how to work and conduct meetings.
>
> - We discussed was that it would be best to avoid taking on a project that was too ambitious, given the limited time we had for the group project.
>
> - What was the correct way to put together the portfolio for this group project, keeping in mind that it was an individual portfolio but that we could create shared documents if they would help provide a clearer picture of the project.

> [!success] What changed
> **Before:** Poor communication, disorganized processes, lack of commitment, irregular attendance
